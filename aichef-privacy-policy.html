Privacy Policy – AI Chef

Last updated: February 27, 2026

AI Chef (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Camera and Images

AI Chef uses your device’s camera to allow you to take photos of food ingredients for analysis.

  • Photos are used only for ingredient recognition and recipe generation
  • Images are processed securely
  • Images are not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used for marketing
  • Images may be:
    • Temporarily processed on our servers
    • Stored for a limited time to enable features such as scan history, if enabled by the user

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited, non-identifiable technical information, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Anonymous usage and performance data

This information is used solely to improve app performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used for the following purposes:

  • Analyze food images and detect ingredients
  • Generate recipe suggestions
  • Maintain scan history (if enabled)
  • Manage premium subscriptions and access
  • Improve app functionality and user experience

3. Third-Party Services

AI Chef uses the following third-party services:

3.1 Google Gemini (Generative AI)

  • Used for image and ingredient analysis
  • Images are processed securely
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training ads

3.2 Supabase

  • Used as a secure backend service
  • Stores anonymized user data and preferences
  • Provides authentication and database services

3.3 RevenueCat

  • Used to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases
  • Does not store sensitive payment information
  • Subscription handling is managed via the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android)

3.4 Apple App Store / Google Play

  • Used for in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Apple or Google handles all billing, payments, and financial information

3.5 Google AdMob

  • Used to display advertisements to free users
  • May collect device identifiers for ad personalization, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice on iOS
  • Premium users enjoy an ad-free experience

3.6 Apple Sign In & Google Sign-In

  • Used for optional account authentication
  • Only your basic profile (name, email) is used to secure your data across devices

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Secure communication channels (HTTPS)
  • Restricted access to backend services
  • No unnecessary data retention

5. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for marketing profiling

Your data is shared only with the services listed above and only to provide app functionality.

6. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data (also available in-app via Account → Delete Account)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

You can contact us using the email below to exercise these rights.

7. Children’s Privacy

AI Chef is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
