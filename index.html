GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

What is AI Chef?+

AI Chef is an AI recipe app and cooking companion that helps you decide what to cook using ingredients you already have, then guides you through making it.

How does ingredient scanning work?+

Take a photo of your ingredients with your camera. AI Chef uses AI to detect ingredients and find matching recipes.

Can I add ingredients manually?+

Yes. You can enter ingredients manually instead of scanning them.

How are recipes matched to my ingredients?+

AI Chef uses the ingredients you scan or enter to help you discover recipes that match what you already have.

Is AI Chef free?+

Yes. You can use AI Chef for free, with optional Premium features available for users who want more.

Which languages are supported?+

AI Chef supports English and Turkish.

Where can I download AI Chef?+

AI Chef is available on Google Play and the App Store.
